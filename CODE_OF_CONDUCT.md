# Code of Conduct

Qasey adopts the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Be kind, accept constructive feedback, respect differing experiences, and keep
discussion focused on the project. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, events,
and other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or
remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security advisory
form. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy and evaluate incidents using
the Contributor Covenant Community Impact Guidelines.
